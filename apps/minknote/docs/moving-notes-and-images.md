## Images

You can drag and drop images directly into a note from your Desktop or anywhere on your filesystem. Pasting images works as well.

Images appear inline in the editor and are stored automatically alongside the note in a folder generated for you. You don't need to manage image files manually.

## Notes

Notes can be moved freely between journals and folders.

To move a note, drag it from the note list (the middle column) and drop it onto any journal or folder in the sidebar.

When you move a note, **all of its associated images move with it automatically**. You never end up with broken image links or orphaned files.

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

* Each note has its own image folder stored next to the Markdown file
* Images are kept in sync with the note at all times
* Moving a note also moves its image folder to the new location

This means you can reorganise your notes as much as you like without worrying about losing or breaking embedded media.
