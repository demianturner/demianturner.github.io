As your collection grows, MinkNote gives you several ways to get back to the information you need. You can search across everything, narrow down what you're already browsing, find text inside a note, or keep important notes close at hand with Favourites and pins.

## Search All Notes

Use **Search All Notes** when you know what you're looking for but not where you put it.

Press `⌘⇧F` to search across all notes in all journals.

Results appear as you type. Use `↑` and `↓` to move through them and press `Return` to open the selected note.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.06.39%402x.png)
This is the quickest way to find something when you don't remember which journal or folder contains it.

## Filter the Notes You're Browsing

Sometimes you already know roughly where a note is. In that case, you can filter the notes you're currently browsing instead of searching everything.

Press `⌘⌥F` and start typing to narrow down the note list.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.11.54%402x.png)
Filtering is useful when you're working within a journal or folder and want to quickly reduce a longer list to a few matching notes.

## Find Text in a Note

To find a word or phrase inside the note you're currently viewing, press `⌘F`.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.15.03%402x.png)
MinkNote highlights matching text so you can move quickly through longer notes without having to scan the whole document.

## Favourites

Use **Favourites** for notes you want to return to regularly.

Mark a note as a favourite and it will appear under **Favourites** in the sidebar, giving you a single place for important notes from across your journals.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.19.44%402x.png)
A note stays in its original journal or folder. Adding it to Favourites simply gives you another quick way to reach it.

## Pinning Notes

Pin a note when you want to keep it at the top of a journal or folder while you're working with it.

Right-click a note and choose **Pin**. Pinned notes appear in a separate **PINNED** section above the other notes, making them easy to reach.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.22.53%402x.png)
Pins are especially useful for notes you're actively working on, reference material you need frequently, or anything you want to keep visible for a while.

To remove a pin, right-click the note again and choose **Pin** to turn it off.

### Pins or Favourites?

They serve slightly different purposes:

* **Pin** a note when you want to keep it prominent within its journal or folder.
* **Favourite** a note when you want quick access to it from anywhere in MinkNote.

A note can be both pinned and a favourite.

## Browse by Tag

Tags let you bring together related notes even when they're stored in different journals or folders.

Select a tag in the **Tags** section of the sidebar to see every note using that tag.
![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/finding-notes/CleanShot_2026-09-29_at_12.26.47%402x.png)
To add or remove tags from the current note, use the **Tags** button (`⌘T`).

Tags are stored with your Markdown notes, so they remain part of your files rather than being locked inside MinkNote.
