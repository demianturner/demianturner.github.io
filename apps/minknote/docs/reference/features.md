## Writing and editing

* **Rich text and Markdown**: Switch between visual editing and Markdown on Mac; edit notes with rich text on iPhone and iPad.
* **Formatting**: Use headings, lists, checklists, tables, quotes, code and a formatting toolbar on Mac.
* **Links**: Add web links or link notes within a project. Note links continue to work when the destination is moved or renamed.
* **Media**: Add images by dragging or pasting on Mac. Preview YouTube and Reddit videos using shortcodes.
* **Writing tools**: Use Apple Writing Tools, editor text zoom and a comfortable writing area on Mac.
* **Saving**: Choose automatic or manual saving on Mac; mobile edits save automatically.

## Organising notes

* **Journals and folders**: Organise notes in journals, folders and subfolders; move notes by drag and drop on Mac.
* **Tags and favourites**: Add tags with suggestions, browse notes by tag, and collect favourites across journals.
* **Pinned notes**: Keep important notes at the top of their journal or folder.
* **Note list**: Sort notes, group dates by month and year, and filter by title, images or tags on Mac.
* **Metadata**: Store tags, dates and other note details in Markdown front matter. Customise journal names, icons and colours.

## Finding and navigating

* **Search**: Search notes across a project, filter the current note list, or find text within a note on Mac.
* **Keyboard navigation**: Use shortcuts to move between panes, find notes and perform common actions. Open the shortcuts panel with `⌘⇧K`.
* **Layouts and windows**: Choose a one-, two- or three-pane Mac layout and open notes in separate windows.

## Projects and files

* **Plain files**: Notes remain Markdown files in folders you can use with other tools. Changes made outside MinkNote are reflected in the app.
* **Projects**: Open and switch between separate collections of journals on Mac. Open existing Markdown folders without importing them.
* **Attachments**: Images are stored alongside notes in `i/` folders and move with their notes on Mac.
* **iCloud**: With Pro, create or move projects into MinkNote’s iCloud folder and open them on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Mac projects can also use other cloud folders.

## Appearance and access

* **Appearance**: Follow the system appearance or choose light or dark mode; customise the Mac interface and journal icons.
* **Accessibility**: Use keyboard navigation and macOS accessibility features, including VoiceOver and reduced motion.
* **Free and Pro**: Free includes editing in one journal; Pro enables editing across projects and iCloud access. See [Free and Pro Access](/apps/minknote/docs/understanding-minknote/free-and-pro-access/).
