MinkNote gives you several ways to find your notes and move around quickly. You can search across everything, filter a journal, find text within a note, or browse using folders and tags.

## Search

There are three ways to search:

* **Search all notes** (`⌘⇧F`) — Search across every journal.
* **Filter the current journal** (`⌘⌥F`) — Quickly narrow down the notes you're currently browsing.
* **Find in the current note** (`⌘F`) — Find text within the note you're reading or editing.

Search works from the keyboard too. Use `↑` and `↓` to move through results and `Return` to open a note.

## Navigate with the Keyboard

You can move around MinkNote without reaching for the mouse:

* `⌃⌥←` / `⌃⌥→` — Move between the sidebar, note list, and editor
* `↑` / `↓` — Move through notes and search results
* `←` / `→` — Collapse or expand folders
* `Return` — Open the selected note or search result

## Sort Your Notes

Use the **Sort** control above the note list to arrange notes by **Title**, **Created**, or **Modified**.

You can reverse the order at any time, for example from A–Z to Z–A, or newest to oldest.

## All Notes, Favourites, and Tags

**All Notes** brings together notes from every journal in one place.

Mark notes you return to often as **Favourites**, or use **tags** to group related notes across different journals and folders.

You'll find more ways to keep important notes close at hand in the **Finding Notes** section of Getting Started.
