Add visual distinction to your journals with custom icons and colours, making it easier to navigate between different areas of your notes at a glance.

## How to Customise

1. **Right-click** on any journal in the sidebar
2. Select **Journal Properties**
3. Choose an **icon** from the gallery
4. Optionally select a **colour** to highlight the icon

![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/howto/CleanShot_2026-02-04_at_16.22.04%402x.png)

## Tips

* Disable journal icons entirely by unticking **Enable Journal Icons** for a cleaner look
* Toggle **Colour Journal Icons** to add colour to the icon itself in addition to the colour stripe
* Both settings apply globally, not just to individual journals
* Choose icons that reflect each journal's purpose (e.g., graduation cap for learning, coffee for work, camera for travel)

> **Note**: Custom icons are only available for journals, not folders. Folders always use the standard folder icon.
