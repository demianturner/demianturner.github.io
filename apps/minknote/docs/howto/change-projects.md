## What is a Project?

A project is simply a folder on your Mac. Switch between projects at any time to work with different sets of notes. Each project maintains its own settings and selection state.

## How to Switch Projects

* Open **Preferences** and navigate to the **Projects** tab
* Click the **Switch Project** button

![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/howto/CleanShot_2026-02-04_at_16.45.15_2%402x.png)

* Choose from your recently opened projects, or click **Browse** to select a new folder

![](/apps/minknote/docs/images/howto/CleanShot_2026-02-04_at_16.48.49_2%402x.png)

## What Gets Preserved?

When switching between projects, MinkNote remembers:

* All project-specific settings
* Your currently selected note and journal
* Window layout and view preferences

This means you can seamlessly switch between different projects without losing your place or reconfiguring settings.
