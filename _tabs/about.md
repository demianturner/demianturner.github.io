## Muse23

Muse23 is an independent software consultancy specialising in native apps and digital products.

With more than 20 years of software development experience, Muse23 provides hands-on engineering from initial ideas through architecture, development and delivery, with particular expertise across Apple platforms.

Alongside consultancy work, Muse23 designs and builds its own [independent apps](/apps/), focusing on useful, thoughtfully made software that solves real problems.

Specialties include Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, AppKit, iOS and macOS development, software architecture, API integration, CI/CD and product development.
